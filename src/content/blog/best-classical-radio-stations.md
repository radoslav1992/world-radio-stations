If you want one recommendation, start with **Classic FM** in the UK for easy, familiar classical music, and **BBC Radio 3** for full concerts and opera. After those, the best classical radio stations online are **WQXR** in New York, **France Musique** in Paris, **BR-Klassik** in Munich, **Radio Swiss Classic** and **ABC Classic** in Australia. They are all free to stream, and you can play every one of them from the [World Radio Stations player](/#player) without signing up.

Below is what each station sounds like, where it broadcasts and how to tune in.

## United Kingdom

### Classic FM

[Classic FM](/stations/classic-fm-uk) went on air on 7 September 1992, opening with Handel’s *Zadok the Priest*. It is owned by Global and plays well-known pieces, film scores and shorter works, with relaxed presenters who explain the music without talking down to you. The best-known event of its year is the **Classic FM Hall of Fame**: listeners vote in January and the top 300 is counted down over Easter. In 2026 Karl Jenkins’ *The Armed Man: A Mass for Peace* reached number one, making him the first living composer to top the chart.

Classic FM broadcasts across the UK on FM between 99.9 and 101.9 MHz, plus DAB and the Global Player app. Stream it live on its [station guide](/stations/classic-fm-uk).

**Best for:** newcomers, background listening and favourite pieces.

### BBC Radio 3

[BBC Radio 3](/stations/bbc-radio-3) launched on 30 September 1967, taking over from the BBC’s Third Programme. It is the more serious choice. You get complete symphonies, opera, chamber music and new commissions, plus jazz, world music and drama. Every summer it broadcasts every concert of the BBC Proms. Radio 3 is on FM between 90.2 and 92.6 MHz, on DAB and on BBC Sounds.

Since September 2025 the BBC has also run **Radio 3 Unwind**, a calmer classical service with a wellbeing focus, on DAB+ and BBC Sounds. For more about the BBC’s networks, see our [BBC radio guide](/blog/bbc-radio-guide).

**Best for:** live concerts, complete works and opera.

### Magic Classical

Bauer launched this national DAB station as Scala Radio on 4 March 2019 and renamed it Magic Classical in September 2024. It is a lighter, commercial alternative to the two big names. Find it on the [United Kingdom page](/country/gb).

**Best for:** a third national classical choice on UK DAB.

## North America

### WQXR (New York)

[WQXR](/stations/wqxr) has used its call letters since 1936. It was once known as the radio station of *The New York Times*, and in October 2009 it moved to 105.9 FM as a non-commercial station run by New York Public Radio. It plays classical music around the clock, from orchestral and choral works to opera and chamber music, and it has close links with New York’s concert halls. Stream it on its [station guide](/stations/wqxr).

**Best for:** a full-time classical station with an American voice.

### WFMT (Chicago)

WFMT on 98.7 FM has played classical music and fine arts programming in Chicago since 1951. Its WFMT Radio Network distributes concert broadcasts to stations across the United States. Find it on the [United States page](/country/us).

**Best for:** concert broadcasts and long-form classical radio.

### KUSC (Los Angeles)

KUSC on 91.5 FM is owned by the University of Southern California and has been on the air since 1946. It is the flagship of the Classical California network. Our guide to [public radio in the USA](/blog/best-public-radio-usa) covers more stations like it.

**Best for:** West Coast listeners and LA Phil fans.

### CBC Music (Canada)

[CBC Music](/stations/cbc-music) is the music network of Canada’s public broadcaster. It began in 1960 as CBC FM, was later called CBC Stereo and then CBC Radio 2, and took its current name in 2018. Today it is a mixed music station: classical mostly fills the middle of the day, and other genres take over at other times. It gives Canadian performers a lot of airtime. The Toronto flagship is on 94.1 FM. See also our [best radio stations in Canada](/blog/best-radio-stations-canada).

**Best for:** classical over lunch, alongside Canadian artists.

## Mainland Europe

### France Musique (France)

France Musique is Radio France’s music network. It started in 1954 and concentrates on classical music and jazz, with many live and as-live concerts, including most of those given by the Orchestre National de France. In Paris it is on 91.7 FM. If you prefer a private station, Radio Classique (101.1 FM in Paris) mixes classical music with news and culture. Find both on the [France page](/country/fr).

**Best for:** live concerts and a mix of classical and jazz.

### BR-Klassik (Germany)

BR-Klassik is the classical network of Bayerischer Rundfunk, Bavaria’s public broadcaster. It started in 1980 as Bayern 4 Klassik and has used its current name since 2009. It broadcasts on FM and DAB+ across Bavaria and streams online. Find it on the [Germany page](/country/de).

**Best for:** classical music from one of Germany’s big public broadcasters.

### Radio Swiss Classic (Switzerland)

Swiss public broadcaster SRG SSR runs Radio Swiss Classic. It is funded by the licence fee, carries no advertising and keeps talk to short introductions between pieces. There are German, French and Italian versions, all on DAB+ in Switzerland and online. Its sister station [Radio Swiss Jazz](/stations/radio-swiss-jazz) works the same way. Find it on the [Switzerland page](/country/ch).

**Best for:** uninterrupted listening while you work or study.

### NPO Klassiek (Netherlands)

The Dutch public classical station began in 1975 as Hilversum 4, was called NPO Radio 4 from 2014, and became NPO Klassiek in 2022. Find it on the [Netherlands page](/country/nl).

**Best for:** classical music from Dutch public radio.

## Australia

### ABC Classic

ABC Classic started on 24 January 1976 as ABC-FM, broadcasting from Adelaide. It became ABC Classic FM in 1994 and ABC Classic in 2019. It is known for its **Classic 100** countdowns, which listeners vote on. Frequencies include 92.9 FM in Sydney, 105.9 FM in Melbourne, 106.1 FM in Brisbane and 97.7 FM in Perth. Find it on the [Australia page](/country/au), and see our [best radio stations in Australia](/blog/best-radio-stations-australia).

**Best for:** listeners in Asia-Pacific time zones and countdown fans.

## FAQ

### What is the best classical radio station?

For accessible, well-known classical music, Classic FM is the most popular choice in the UK. For complete concerts, opera and the BBC Proms, BBC Radio 3 is the stronger pick. Outside the UK, WQXR, France Musique, BR-Klassik and ABC Classic are all excellent.

### What frequency is Classic FM UK on?

Classic FM broadcasts on FM between 99.9 and 101.9 MHz, depending on where you are in the UK. It is also on DAB digital radio and online. You can stream it on its [station guide](/stations/classic-fm-uk).

### Can I listen to classical radio stations online for free?

Yes. Every station in this list streams free online, and you can play them from the [World Radio Stations player](/#player) with no account.

### Which classical station has the least talk?

Radio Swiss Classic keeps speech to short introductions and carries no advertising, which makes it one of the least interrupted classical stations online.
