No single chart ranks every radio station in the world. Each country runs its own audience survey, and they don't count listeners the same way. Country by country, though, the leaders are clear. **BBC Radio 2** is the UK's most-listened-to station, with about 12.4 million listeners a week. **France Inter** leads in France, **Cadena SER** in Spain, **RMF FM** in Poland and **RTL 102.5** in Italy. The biggest international audience we can source belongs to the **BBC World Service**, at 355 million people a week across all its languages and platforms.

Below are the most popular and famous radio stations around the world, region by region, with sourced figures where they exist. Every one streams free in our [player](/#player).

## How "popular" is measured

Radio audiences are counted country by country, usually by an industry body that surveys a sample of listeners:

- **United Kingdom:** RAJAR publishes weekly reach, hours and share of listening every quarter.
- **United States:** Nielsen measures audiences market by market, so no single national figure exists for most stations.
- **France:** Médiamétrie reports the share of people who tune in to a station on an average day.
- **Spain:** the EGM survey counts daily listeners and is released in "waves" through the year.
- **Italy:** the Audiradio survey counts listeners on an average day. It replaced the older TER survey, so new and old numbers aren't directly comparable.
- **Germany:** the ma Audio survey (Media-Analyse) reports listeners in an average hour.
- **Poland:** Radio Track reports each station's share of listening time.

These metrics answer different questions. **Reach** is how many people listen at least once in a day or week, and weekly reach is always bigger than daily reach. **Share** is the slice of all listening time a station wins. Population matters too, and network brands like Heart and Capital add up many local stations and spin-offs.

## United Kingdom

In RAJAR's second-quarter 2026 survey, a record 51.1 million adults (about 87% of people aged 15 and over) listened each week.

- **[BBC Radio 2](/stations/bbc-radio-2)** is the UK's most-listened-to single station, with 12.37 million weekly listeners. It mixes pop and adult contemporary music with entertainment shows.
- **[BBC Radio 4](/stations/bbc-radio-4)**, the BBC's speech network and home of the *Today* programme, reached 8.9 million people a week.
- **[BBC Radio 1](/stations/bbc-radio-1)** launched in 1967 as the BBC's answer to the pop pirate stations, and it is still the corporation's station for new music.
- **[Heart](/stations/heart-fm)** and **[Capital](/stations/capital-fm)** are two of Global's biggest networks. Global puts the Heart brand at more than 12.8 million weekly listeners, and Capital reached close to 9 million.
- **[talkSPORT](/stations/talksport)** is Britain's biggest commercial sports station. In the same survey its breakfast show, with about 1.4 million listeners, pulled ahead of BBC Radio 5 Live's.
- **[LBC](/stations/lbc)** was the UK's first legal commercial station in 1973 and is now a national talk network. **[Classic FM](/stations/classic-fm-uk)** is the country's most popular classical station.

For frequencies, see our guide to [UK radio stations and frequencies](/blog/uk-radio-stations-frequencies), and for the BBC line-up, read our [BBC Radio guide](/blog/bbc-radio-guide). You'll find hundreds more British stations on the [United Kingdom page](/country/gb).

## The BBC World Service: a global audience

The [BBC World Service](/stations/bbc-world-service) started in 1932 as the Empire Service, broadcasting on shortwave. It now produces news in English and dozens of other languages. In figures published in July 2026, the BBC said World Service output reached 355 million people a week across radio, TV and online. That was more than 70% of the 502 million people the BBC reaches outside the UK.

Outside the UK, note that the BBC closed its BBC Sounds app to international users in July 2025 and now points them to BBC.com; you can also stream the World Service from its guide page.

## United States and Canada

Most American radio stations are local. Each one is licensed to a city or town, and Nielsen reports ratings market by market. The best-known national name is public radio's NPR.

- **[NPR](/stations/npr)** was incorporated in 1970, and *All Things Considered* premiered in May 1971. More than 1,000 member stations carry its programmes. NPR says its broadcast programming reaches 21.2 million people a week, and about 46 million across all platforms (2025).
- Some of NPR's best-known member stations are [WNYC](/stations/wnyc) in New York (93.9 FM and 820 AM), [KQED](/stations/kqed) in San Francisco, one of the largest public radio audiences in the US, and [WBEZ](/stations/wbez) in Chicago, the station where *This American Life* began.
- On the commercial side, New York's **[Z100](/stations/z100-new-york)** (100.3 FM) is one of the best-known Top 40 brands in the country, and **[Hot 97](/stations/hot-97)** (97.1 FM) is a landmark of hip hop radio.
- Seattle's **[KEXP](/stations/kexp)** is famous far beyond its city thanks to its filmed live sessions.

In Canada, the public broadcaster's **[CBC Radio One](/stations/cbc-radio-one)** is the national news and talk network. **[ICI Radio-Canada Première](/stations/ici-premiere)** serves French-speaking listeners, and **[CBC Music](/stations/cbc-music)** covers music. For more, see the [best public radio stations in the US](/blog/best-public-radio-usa), [top US radio stations](/blog/top-us-radio-stations-online) and the [best radio stations in Canada](/blog/best-radio-stations-canada).

## France, Spain, Italy and Portugal

- **France:** **[France Inter](/stations/france-inter)** is France's most-listened-to station. In Médiamétrie's April–June 2026 survey, 12.1% of people tuned in on an average weekday, ahead of RTL (8.5%) and franceinfo (8.3%). Radio France's **[FIP](/stations/fip)** has a devoted following far beyond France, and **[NRJ](/stations/nrj-france)**, launched in Paris in 1981, is one of the country's leading hit-music brands.
- **Spain:** **[Cadena SER](/stations/cadena-ser)**, whose roots go back to 1924, topped the second 2026 wave of the EGM survey with about 4.1 million daily listeners. **[COPE](/stations/cadena-cope)** came second with about 3.4 million, and **[Onda Cero](/stations/onda-cero)** is the third big talk network. **[LOS40](/stations/los40)**, founded in 1966 as Los 40 Principales, is the top music station, with about 2.7 million daily listeners.
- **Italy:** **[RTL 102.5](/stations/rtl-102-5)** says the Audiradio survey gave it about 6.3 million listeners on an average day in Q2 2026. **[Rai Radio 1](/stations/rai-radio-1)**, the public news and sport channel, had about 3.1 million in Audiradio's Q3 2025 survey. **[Radio Deejay](/stations/radio-deejay)** is a long-running pop and entertainment favourite.
- **Portugal:** **[Rádio Comercial](/stations/radio-comercial)** has been the country's most-listened-to station in recent years, helped by its popular morning show.

## Central and Northern Europe

- **Poland:** **[RMF FM](/stations/rmf-fm)**, founded in Kraków in 1990, took 28.7% of all radio listening time in the March–May 2026 Radio Track survey. That is about twice the share of its rival **[Radio ZET](/stations/radio-zet)**.
- **Germany:** German radio is organised by region, so there is no single national giant. Big names include WDR's youth station **[1LIVE](/stations/1live-wdr)**, **[SWR3](/stations/swr3)**, Bavaria's **[Antenne Bayern](/stations/antenne-bayern)** and **[Bayern 3](/stations/bayern-3)**, and the national news station **[Deutschlandfunk](/stations/deutschlandfunk)**. In the ma 2026 Audio II survey, Bayern 1 fell below one million listeners in an average hour. Until then, it had been the only German station above that mark.
- **Austria, Switzerland and the Low Countries:** **[Hitradio Ö3](/stations/oe3)** has led Austrian radio for decades. **[SRF 3](/stations/srf-3)** is a favourite in German-speaking Switzerland. In the Netherlands, the big names are **[NPO Radio 2](/stations/npo-radio-2)**, famous for its *Top 2000* countdown, and the commercial rivals **[Qmusic](/stations/qmusic)** and **[Radio 538](/stations/radio-538)**. Belgium's **[Studio Brussel](/stations/studio-brussel)** is a leading alternative station.
- **Scandinavia:** **[NRK P1](/stations/nrk-p1)** is Norway's number one. Radionytt put its daily reach at 20.6% in July 2025, more than double the main P4 channel. In Sweden, **[Sveriges Radio P4](/stations/sveriges-radio-p4)** has the biggest audience, while **[P3](/stations/sveriges-radio-p3)** is the public broadcaster's youth channel.
- **Ireland:** **[RTÉ Radio 1](/stations/rte-radio-1)**, which began in 1926 as 2RN, is the country's flagship. Ireland also has national commercial stations such as **Today FM** and **Newstalk**; find them on the [Ireland page](/country/ie).

## Australia, New Zealand, Africa and Latin America

- **Australia:** the ABC's national youth station **[triple j](/stations/triple-j)** is known for championing new music, and its listener-voted Hottest 100 is a national event. Sydney's **[Nova 96.9](/stations/nova-969)** and **[smoothfm](/stations/smoothfm)** are big names on the commercial side. See the [best radio stations in Australia](/blog/best-radio-stations-australia).
- **New Zealand:** **[RNZ National](/stations/rnz-national)** is the public news and speech network, on 101 FM nationwide.
- **South Africa:** the SABC's isiZulu station **Ukhozi FM** has long led the national ratings. In 2021 the SABC cited BRC RAMS data showing it with about 8 million weekly listeners. The same data put **[Metro FM](/stations/metro-fm)** at about 5 million, making it the most-listened-to commercial station. The SABC also runs **[5FM](/stations/5fm)**. Browse them all on the [South Africa page](/country/za).
- **Latin America:** LOS40 has sister stations in several Spanish-speaking countries, and in Chile a good place to start is the news and talk station **Radio Cooperativa**. Explore more on the [Chile page](/country/cl).

## FAQ

### What is the most popular radio station in the world?

There's no official global ranking. By audience, the BBC World Service is the largest international broadcaster we can source, reaching 355 million people a week across all its languages and platforms (BBC, 2026). Among national stations, the biggest audiences we can source include BBC Radio 2 in the UK (about 12.4 million a week, RAJAR Q2 2026) and RTL 102.5 in Italy (about 6.3 million on an average day).

### What are the most famous radio stations?

The BBC World Service, BBC Radio 1 and Radio 2, NPR, France Inter, FIP, Cadena SER, LOS40, triple j and New York's Z100 and Hot 97 are among the best-known names. Most of them have a guide in our [stations directory](/stations).

### What are the best radio stations worldwide?

It depends on what you like. For news, try the BBC World Service or NPR. For music discovery, try KEXP, FIP or triple j. For pop hits, try Capital, LOS40 or RTL 102.5. All of them stream free in our [player](/#player).
