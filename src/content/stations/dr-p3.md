DR P3 is the youth and pop channel of Danmarks Radio, Denmark's public broadcaster. Launched in 1963 and reinvented by every generation since, it mixes new music with the irreverent comedy and satire that Danes consider practically a birthright of the channel.

## History

P3 went on air in 1963 as DR's third programme, created — like so many European pop channels — to answer the offshore pirate stations luring young listeners away from public radio. It became the dominant sound of Danish youth for decades, and even after commercial competition arrived in the 1980s it kept its central role by doubling down on what rivals couldn't copy: fearless satire, personality DJs and a genuine commitment to Danish music. Its talent programme "Karrierekanonen" and the annual "P3 Guld" awards have pushed wave after wave of Danish acts toward stardom.

## Format and programming

Days blend current pop, hip hop and Danish releases with news for young audiences and the comedy segments that routinely cross over into national catchphrases. Evenings go deeper into new and alternative music, while sports coverage — especially national team football and handball — brings the whole country to P3 on big nights.

## Reach

P3 broadcasts nationwide on FM and DAB+ and streams through DR's platforms, remaining among Denmark's most-listened stations despite the streaming era's pressures on youth radio everywhere.

## Significance

P3 is the model of a public youth channel that stayed relevant: it launches Danish artists, trains Danish broadcasting's next generation, and has shaped the national sense of humour for sixty years.
