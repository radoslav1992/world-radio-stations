102.7 KIIS-FM is Los Angeles' leading contemporary hit radio station and one of the most influential pop stations in America. Broadcasting to the second-largest radio market in the country, it has been a launching pad for pop hits and radio careers for over four decades.

## History

The KIIS call letters settled on 102.7 FM in Los Angeles in the mid-1970s, but the station's golden era began in 1981 when Rick Dees arrived to host mornings. Under Dees, KIIS became the highest-billing radio station in the United States during the 1980s, and his "Weekly Top 40" countdown, launched from the station, became a global syndication phenomenon. Ryan Seacrest took over the morning show in 2004, broadcasting from KIIS in parallel with his television career, and the station has remained the pop flagship of what is now iHeartMedia's national network.

## Format and programming

KIIS plays a tight rotation of current pop, dance and rhythmic hits aimed at a young mainstream audience. "On Air with Ryan Seacrest" anchors the schedule, complemented by high-profile afternoon and evening personalities and syndicated iHeart programming. The station's annual Wango Tango concert, staged since 1998, brings A-list pop line-ups to Los Angeles venues and is broadcast and streamed nationally.

## Reach

The station broadcasts on 102.7 FM across the Los Angeles basin and streams worldwide through iHeartRadio. Its ratings and advertising revenue have kept it among America's top-performing music stations for most of its existence.

## Significance

KIIS-FM defined the sound and business model of American Top 40 FM radio in the post-disco era. Its morning-show model — a celebrity host with one foot in television — and its event franchises have been copied across the industry, and for pop labels a KIIS add remains one of the most valuable airplay commitments in the country.
