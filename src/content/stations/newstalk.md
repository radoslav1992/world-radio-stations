Newstalk is Ireland's only national independent talk station, competing head-on with the public broadcaster RTÉ for the country's news, current-affairs and sport audience. Sharp-elbowed and conversational, it has carved a permanent place in a market long dominated by the state broadcaster.

## History

Newstalk launched in April 2002 as a Dublin talk station and won a national licence in 2006, expanding countrywide with backing from businessman Denis O'Brien's Communicorp. Its statement moment came in 2013, when Pat Kenny — one of RTÉ's biggest names — defected to host mornings, the most significant presenter move in modern Irish broadcasting. Bauer Media Audio acquired the station with the rest of Communicorp's portfolio in 2021.

## Format and programming

Weekdays run from an agenda-setting breakfast through Kenny's mid-morning mix of policy and conversation, lunchtime news, and opinionated drive-time programming. The station's crown jewel is "Off the Ball", its nightly sports show, whose irreverent, essayistic style has won international admiration and repeatedly been named among the best sports programs in the world. Weekend documentaries and business programming deepen the offer.

## Reach

Newstalk broadcasts on FM across the Republic of Ireland and streams globally, with Off the Ball's podcast catalogue giving it a following well beyond Irish shores.

## Significance

Newstalk broke RTÉ's effective monopoly on Irish speech radio and keeps the national conversation competitive. Its sports desk, in particular, changed expectations of what sports talk can be — literate, funny and genuinely journalistic.
