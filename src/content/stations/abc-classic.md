ABC Classic is Australia's national classical music station, run by the public broadcaster ABC. Relaxed and warmly presented, it aims classical music at everyone — from devotees to listeners who simply want something beautiful on — and holds a unique place in Australian musical life.

## History

The network launched in January 1976 as ABC-FM, the corporation's first FM service and for years the only way most Australians could hear classical music in high fidelity. Renamed ABC Classic FM and eventually ABC Classic, it has been the broadcast partner of Australian orchestras throughout its life — a relationship rooted in the ABC's remarkable history of having founded the country's symphony orchestras in the mid-twentieth century. Its annual listener-voted "Classic 100" countdown, running since 2001, has become a national musical event.

## Format and programming

Days move from gentle breakfast classics through mornings built around major works, to concert broadcasts from Australian and international stages in the evenings. The station records and premieres Australian composition, showcases the country's performers, and leans into accessible presentation — its "Swoon" segments of the most soothing repertoire became so popular they spawned albums.

## Reach

ABC Classic broadcasts on FM in cities and regions across Australia, on DAB+ and via the ABC listen app, with a dedicated second stream, ABC Classic 2, for uninterrupted music.

## Significance

ABC Classic is the backbone of classical music broadcasting in Australia and one of the format's most successful public stations anywhere — proof that a national classical network can be beloved rather than merely respected.
