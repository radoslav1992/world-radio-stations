Today FM is Ireland's leading independent music station — a national broadcaster with the feel of a well-loved local one, built on music, wit and presenters Irish listeners treat as old friends.

## History

The station launched on St Patrick's Day 1997 as Radio Ireland, only the second national commercial licence in the country's history. A rocky first year prompted a relaunch as Today FM in early 1998, and the arrival of Ian Dempsey to host breakfast steadied the ship — his show, with Mario Rosenstock's "Gift Grub" comedy sketches, became one of Irish radio's most durable institutions and still leads the schedule more than a quarter-century later. After years under Communicorp, the station joined Bauer Media Audio Ireland in 2021.

## Format and programming

Today FM plays adult-leaning pop and rock with a strong Irish accent — literal and musical — supporting homegrown artists alongside international hits. Personality is the point: Dempsey's breakfast, Dave Moore's daytime mischief and Matt Cooper's "The Last Word", the drive-time news and current-affairs show that gives the music station serious journalistic weight. Comedy, live sessions and the station's charity fundraising drives (its "Dare to Care" campaigns are annual fixtures) round out the identity.

## Reach

Today FM broadcasts on FM nationwide across the Republic of Ireland and streams internationally, consistently ranking among the country's most-listened stations with a weekly audience approaching a million in a nation of five million.

## Significance

Today FM proved a national commercial station could compete with the mighty RTÉ on charm rather than budget. Its balance of music, comedy and one serious daily news program has become a template for independent radio in small markets.
