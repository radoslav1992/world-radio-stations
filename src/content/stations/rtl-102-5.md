RTL 102.5 is Italy's foremost private national radio station, broadcasting contemporary hit music, news and entertainment around the clock. For many years it has ranked at or near the top of Italian radio audience surveys, making it one of the most influential commercial broadcasters in the country.

## History

The station began in 1975 as a local broadcaster in the Bergamo area of Lombardy, in the wave of "free radio" stations that emerged after Italian court rulings opened the airwaves to private operators. Originally known as Radio Trasmissioni Lombarde, it expanded steadily through the 1980s and 1990s from a regional outlet into a true national network, adopting the RTL 102.5 name that reflects its signature FM frequency. It later became a pioneer of "radiovision" in Italy, simulcasting its radio studios as a television channel.

## Format and programming

RTL 102.5 programmes a broad contemporary hit and hot adult contemporary playlist, mixing current international and Italian chart music with recent favourites. The schedule combines music-driven shows with regular news bulletins, traffic information, sport and talk segments featuring listener interaction. Its slogan, "Very Normal People", emphasises the station's accessible, mainstream identity. The brand has also expanded into spin-off digital channels serving more specialised music tastes.

## Reach

The station broadcasts nationwide across Italy on FM, principally around 102.5 MHz, as well as on DAB+ digital radio and via digital and satellite television platforms that carry its radiovision feed. It also streams online and through its own apps.

## Significance

RTL 102.5's rise from a local Lombard station to Italy's most-listened radio brand illustrates the growth of private broadcasting in Italy since the 1970s. Its combination of national FM coverage, television simulcasting and digital distribution has made it a model for multi-platform commercial radio in Europe.
