CKNW 980 is the leading news and talk station of Vancouver and British Columbia. For most of its history the self-proclaimed "Top Dog" of west coast radio, it has set the agenda of BC public life — the station premiers listen to, and worry about.

## History

CKNW signed on in 1944 in New Westminster — the "NW" of its call letters — and moved to the centre of Vancouver life as the city grew. Through the postwar decades it built a formidable news operation and a stable of talk hosts with real political weight; a morning interview on CKNW could make or break a provincial career. The station was also the longtime radio home of the Vancouver Canucks, whose broadcasts anchored its evenings for decades. Owned today by Corus Entertainment, it operates alongside the Global News television brand.

## Format and programming

CKNW's weekday spine is news through morning drive, followed by talk programming on BC politics, housing, transit and the issues of a fast-changing region, with open-line segments that remain genuinely influential. Weekend schedules mix gardening, money and lifestyle talk in the classic full-service tradition.

## Reach

The station broadcasts on 980 AM across the Lower Mainland, with a signal reaching Vancouver Island and into Washington State, and streams through Global's platforms.

## Significance

CKNW is one of the great regional agenda-setters of Canadian broadcasting. Its journalism and phone lines have driven British Columbia's public conversation for eighty years, and its heritage — from Expo 86 coverage to Canucks playoff runs — is inseparable from Vancouver's own.
