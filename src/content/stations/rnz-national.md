RNZ National is the flagship radio network of Radio New Zealand, the country's public service broadcaster. Commercial-free and funded by the state, it provides news, current affairs, documentaries and cultural programming to a nationwide audience.

## History

RNZ National descends from New Zealand's earliest state broadcasting services, which developed through the twentieth century under successive public broadcasting organisations. The network was long known as National Radio before adopting the Radio New Zealand National name, and later the shortened RNZ branding as the organisation repositioned itself as a multiplatform public media outlet. Throughout these changes it has remained the country's principal spoken-word radio service, with its operations centred in Wellington and a significant presence in Auckland.

## Format and programming

The network's schedule is dominated by news and current affairs, including the influential breakfast programme Morning Report and the late-afternoon Checkpoint. Between the news blocks it carries interview-driven magazine shows, science and rural affairs coverage, documentaries, drama and music features. Programming reflects RNZ's charter obligations, including coverage of Māori issues and use of te reo Māori alongside English. Much of the network's output is distributed as podcasts and on-demand audio.

## Reach

RNZ National broadcasts across New Zealand on FM around 101 MHz and on AM frequencies, giving it some of the most comprehensive geographic coverage of any broadcaster in the country. It also streams live online and reaches international audiences through the RNZ website and apps.

## Significance

As New Zealand's public radio network of record, RNZ National holds a central place in the nation's media. It is a primary source of independent journalism, an official channel for emergency and civil defence information, and a long-standing platform for New Zealand voices, stories and music.
