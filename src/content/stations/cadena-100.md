Cadena 100 is one of Spain's largest music radio networks, playing melodic pop hits — Spanish and international — for a broad adult audience. It is the flagship music brand of the COPE group, giving the Church-linked broadcaster a cheerful, chart-friendly counterpart to its talk network.

## History

Cadena 100 was assembled in the early 1990s, when the COPE group unified its FM music frequencies under a single national brand. It found its lasting identity in the 2000s as a "feel-good" hits network, powered by one of Spanish music radio's most successful morning shows — "¡Buenos días, Javi y Mar!", whose warm, family-friendly comedy has led the format's ratings for years. The network sits today within Ábside Media alongside COPE, Rock FM and MegaStar FM.

## Format and programming

The playlist favours melodic pop from the past two decades with a healthy share of Spanish artists, avoiding harder edges — the station's promise is buen rollo, good vibes. Beyond the morning show, the schedule runs personality-led dayparts, countdowns and heavy artist involvement: its annual "Cadena 100 Por Ellas" concert in Madrid, supporting breast cancer research, gathers the cream of Spanish pop each autumn and has become a fixture of the music calendar.

## Reach

Cadena 100 broadcasts on FM across Spain through owned stations and affiliates, and streams via its app, ranking consistently among the country's top music networks by audience.

## Significance

Cadena 100 demonstrates how Spain's radio groups pair talk flagships with complementary music brands. Its mix of melodic conservatism and big-hearted events has made it one of the most stable formats in Spanish broadcasting.
