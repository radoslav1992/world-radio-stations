Sveriges Radio P4 is the fourth national channel of Sweden's public broadcaster Sveriges Radio and the country's most listened-to radio channel. It is built around a network of local stations covering every part of Sweden, which broadcast regional news and programming during the day and share a common national schedule at other times.

## History

P4 was established in 1987 as the umbrella channel for Sveriges Radio's local stations, which had been developed across the country during the 1970s and 1980s. The channel brought local broadcasting together with national sports and entertainment output under a single network identity. Over time P4 consolidated its position as the broadest of Sveriges Radio's channels, aimed at a general adult audience, and became the clear market leader in Swedish radio listening.

## Format and programming

P4 operates as a full-service channel. Its 25 local stations, from P4 Norrbotten in the far north to P4 Malmöhus in the south, produce regional news, weather, traffic and local-interest programming for their areas. Nationally, the channel carries news from the Sveriges Radio newsroom, popular entertainment and talk shows, and is the home of Radiosporten, Sweden's main radio sports coverage, including extensive live football and ice hockey commentary. The music mix is broad and adult-oriented, spanning Swedish and international pop and classic hits. P4 also plays a formal role in Sweden's emergency preparedness system, distributing important public announcements.

## Reach

Through its local station network, P4 achieves near-universal FM coverage of Sweden. It is also available on DAB+ in parts of the country and online through the Sveriges Radio Play app and website. Audience surveys consistently show P4 with the largest share of Swedish radio listening.

## Significance

P4 is the backbone of Swedish public radio: the channel closest to everyday local life, the national platform for sport, and a key channel for crisis information. Its combination of local presence and national reach makes it central to Sveriges Radio's public service mission.
