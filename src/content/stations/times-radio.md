Times Radio is a national digital speech station from News UK, built on the journalism of The Times and The Sunday Times. Launched into the middle of the pandemic news cycle, it set out to offer intelligent, courteous news talk — expert conversation rather than confrontation.

## History

Times Radio went on air on 29 June 2020, opening with an interview with the then prime minister. News UK conceived the station both as a showcase for its newspapers' journalists and as a commercial answer to BBC Radio 4 — ad-light, subscription-adjacent and aimed at listeners who wanted depth without the phone-in combat of traditional talk radio. It recruited heavily from the BBC, bringing across established presenters and correspondents, and has steadily built its audience on DAB and online while its YouTube presence — driven by long-form interviews on politics and world affairs — has grown into one of the largest of any British radio station.

## Format and programming

The schedule is rolling news and conversation: extended interviews with politicians, columnists and correspondents, review of the papers, and analysis that draws directly on The Times' newsroom. The tone is deliberately measured, and advertising is kept minimal, with the station serving partly as a marketing engine for Times subscriptions.

## Reach

Times Radio broadcasts UK-wide on DAB and streams globally via its app and website; its digital clips reach an international audience well beyond conventional radio measurement, particularly for coverage of the war in Ukraine and US politics.

## Significance

Times Radio is the most serious newspaper-led challenge yet mounted to the BBC's dominance of British speech radio, and an instructive experiment in what news brands can build in audio when they treat radio and video as one product.
