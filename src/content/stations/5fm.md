5FM is the South African Broadcasting Corporation’s national youth station, built around Top 40 music. Its main FM frequencies are 98.0 FM in Johannesburg, 89.0 FM in Cape Town and 89.9 FM in Durban, with 91.6 FM in Bloemfontein, 89.2 FM in Gqeberha and 88.5 FM in East London. Outside its transmitter areas you can [stream it live](/#player) online.

## History

5FM traces its roots to LM Radio, the commercial station that had broadcast to young South African listeners from Lourenço Marques (now Maputo) in Mozambique. After Mozambique’s independence, the new Frelimo government closed LM Radio, and on 13 October 1975, the day it went silent, the South African government had the SABC take over its staff and service as Radio 5. Nick Megans presented the first live show from the SABC’s Broadcast House on Commissioner Street.

Radio 5 later moved to FM stereo and took the name 5FM, which it has used ever since. The station is still owned and run by the SABC.

## Format and programming

5FM plays a Top 40 format aimed at a young audience: current hits and new releases, with news and sports coverage alongside the music. Programmes come from Johannesburg and are relayed nationally through a network of FM repeaters in the main cities.

Where its sister station [Metro FM](/stations/metro-fm) focuses on urban music for listeners in their mid-twenties to mid-thirties, 5FM plays chart pop for a younger crowd. If you want a mix of the two, use the [South Africa page](/country/za) to switch between South African stations in one player.

## Frequencies and how to listen

5FM uses different frequencies in each region. These figures come from the station’s own frequency finder and independent transmitter listings:

| Province | City or transmitter | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Gauteng | Johannesburg (Sentech Tower) | 98.0 FM |
| Gauteng | Pretoria (Lukasrand) | 103.6 FM |
| Western Cape | Cape Town | 89.0 FM |
| Western Cape | Cape Town – Table Mountain | 89.9 FM |
| KwaZulu-Natal | Durban (Alverstone) | 89.9 FM |
| KwaZulu-Natal | Durban North | 103.8 FM |
| Eastern Cape | Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth) | 89.2 FM |
| Eastern Cape | East London | 88.5 FM |
| Free State | Bloemfontein | 91.6 FM |
| Northern Cape | Alexander Bay | 92.2 FM |

The Johannesburg transmitter on 98.0 FM is also listed for Pretoria, and Durban’s 89.9 FM covers Pietermaritzburg as well. We could not confirm 5FM frequencies for Mpumalanga, Limpopo, North West or the rest of the Northern Cape, so they are not listed.

The station’s FAQ says that, as an SABC service, it is allocated transmitters in certain areas only. If you are outside the cities above, or the signal is poor, the online stream is the reliable option. Press play in the player on this page, or use the 5FM mobile app.

## Significance

5FM is a direct line from LM Radio to today’s SABC. Taking over LM Radio’s staff and service in 1975 gave the public broadcaster its own station for young listeners, and 5FM has kept that role for more than five decades. Alongside Metro FM, it lets the SABC reach younger audiences nationally with two distinct sounds: chart pop on 5FM and urban music on Metro FM.
