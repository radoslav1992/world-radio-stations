Rai Radio 1 is the principal radio channel of RAI, Italy's public service broadcaster. It is the network's main outlet for news, current affairs, sport and public-interest talk, and it traces its lineage directly back to the very beginning of Italian broadcasting.

## History

Regular radio broadcasting in Italy began on 6 October 1924 under the URI (Unione Radiofonica Italiana), the direct ancestor of today's RAI. After the Second World War the public broadcaster reorganised its radio output into national networks, and the 1975 reform of RAI established the modern channel structure in which Radio 1 became the corporation's first and most news-oriented network. Over the decades it has served as the reference station for major national events, elections and breaking news in Italy.

## Format and programming

Rai Radio 1 is built around hourly news bulletins from RAI's radio newsroom (GR1), extended morning and drive-time news magazines, political interviews, and discussion programmes covering economics, society and culture. Sport is a signature strength: the long-running programme "Tutto il calcio minuto per minuto", offering live minute-by-minute football coverage, is one of the most storied titles in Italian radio. Music and entertainment segments round out the schedule, but journalism remains the channel's core identity.

## Reach

The channel is distributed nationwide on FM across Italy, on DAB+ digital radio, via digital television platforms, and online through RAI's RaiPlay Sound streaming and podcast service, which also makes much of its programming available on demand.

## Significance

As the news flagship of Italy's public broadcaster, Rai Radio 1 occupies a central place in the country's media landscape. It is a primary source of radio journalism for Italian listeners and, together with its sister channels Radio 2 and Radio 3, forms the backbone of public service radio in Italy.
