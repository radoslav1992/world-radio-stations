KIIS 106.5 is Sydney's leading hit music station and the home of the most successful — and most talked-about — radio show in Australia: The Kyle & Jackie O Show. Borrowing its brand from the famous Los Angeles station, it has dominated Sydney FM since its 2014 relaunch.

## History

The 106.5 frequency spent years as the adult contemporary "Mix 106.5" before owner ARN pulled off the biggest coup in modern Australian radio: signing Kyle Sandilands and Jackie "O" Henderson away from rival 2Day FM, where they had ruled Sydney breakfast for a decade. The station relaunched as KIIS 106.5 in January 2014 — licensing the KIIS brand from iHeartMedia — and its new breakfast show took the market lead almost immediately, holding it with rare interruption ever since.

## Format and programming

Kyle & Jackie O's mixture of celebrity access, confession and cheerful outrage anchors the station; the pair's 2024 contract extension, reported at around $200 million across a decade, was national front-page news. Around the breakfast juggernaut, KIIS plays current pop and rhythmic hits with high-profile drive programming and heavy involvement in Sydney's entertainment scene.

## Reach

KIIS broadcasts at 106.5 FM across Sydney, on DAB+ and via iHeartRadio, while the KIIS brand extends to sister stations in Melbourne and other capitals — with the Kyle & Jackie O show itself now simulcast into Melbourne.

## Significance

KIIS 106.5 shows how completely a single show can define a station: one signing rebuilt the Sydney FM market. It is also the flagship of ARN's national KIIS network and the reference point for CHR radio in Australia.
