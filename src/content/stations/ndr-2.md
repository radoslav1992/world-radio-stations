NDR 2 is the pop music channel of Norddeutscher Rundfunk, the public broadcaster serving Germany's north. From Hamburg it reaches across Lower Saxony, Schleswig-Holstein and Mecklenburg-Vorpommern with a polished mix of hits, news and humour that has made it one of the country's biggest stations.

## History

NDR 2 grew out of NDR's second radio programme, which through the 1960s and 70s absorbed the era's appetite for pop until the network was formally repositioned as a full-time popular music wave. Serving one of Germany's largest regional audiences, it became the north's default daytime companion, weathering the arrival of private competitors in the 1980s by sharpening its format while keeping public-service news at its core.

## Format and programming

The playlist blends current pop with hits of recent decades, wrapped around strong morning and afternoon shows, regular news from NDR's respected newsroom, and the comedy sketches — a proud NDR 2 tradition — that punctuate the day. The station also champions new music through its "NDR 2 Soundcheck" festival in Göttingen, where emerging acts play alongside established names, and its live sessions bring international stars into its Hamburg studios.

## Reach

NDR 2 broadcasts on FM throughout the four northern federal states, on DAB+ and online, reaching millions of daily listeners and regularly ranking among Germany's top handful of stations by audience.

## Significance

NDR 2 exemplifies the German public "Popwelle": mainstream in sound but public in substance, holding mass audiences that most European public broadcasters lost to commercial radio long ago. For the north of Germany it is simply the sound of the working day.
