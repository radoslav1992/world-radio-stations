Newstalk ZB is New Zealand's leading news and talk network and, by most measures, the country's most-listened commercial radio brand. Its hosts — above all breakfast's Mike Hosking — occupy a central, often combative place in the national conversation.

## History

The ZB name is the oldest brand in New Zealand commercial radio: the original 1ZB, 2ZB, 3ZB and 4ZB stations formed the state-run commercial network that dominated Kiwi listening from the 1930s. In 1987, 1ZB in Auckland flipped to a full news and talk format as Newstalk 1ZB, and the format was progressively networked nationwide through the 1990s. Privatized with Radio New Zealand's commercial arm and eventually landing with NZME, Newstalk ZB has topped the national commercial ratings for most of the past three decades.

## Format and programming

The day is built on personality talk anchored in a large news operation: the Mike Hosking Breakfast — perennially New Zealand's biggest commercial radio show — followed by Kerre Woodham's mornings, afternoon programming, and Heather du Plessis-Allan at drive. Weekend and overnight talkback keeps the phone lines as central to the format as they were in the 1980s, and sports coverage ties into stablemate networks.

## Reach

Newstalk ZB broadcasts on AM and FM frequencies covering virtually all of New Zealand and streams via iHeartRadio, with its interviews clipped constantly into the country's news cycle.

## Significance

Newstalk ZB is the agenda-setter of New Zealand broadcasting: prime ministers front its studios weekly, and its blend of heritage brand, big personalities and nationwide reach has made it the benchmark for talk radio in the country for over thirty years.
