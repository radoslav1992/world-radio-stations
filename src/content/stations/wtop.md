WTOP is the all-news radio station of Washington, D.C., delivering traffic, weather, politics and breaking news around the clock to one of the most news-hungry audiences in the world. It is regularly the top-billing radio station in the entire United States — a remarkable feat for a news outlet in the streaming era.

## History

WTOP's lineage reaches back to the earliest decades of American broadcasting, including years as a CBS-owned powerhouse on 1500 AM. The station adopted the all-news format in 1969, making it one of the country's first, and built its reputation on Washington's insatiable appetite for information. In 2006 WTOP made the then-unusual move of shifting its primary signal to 103.5 FM, betting that news listeners would follow to the FM band — they did, and ratings grew. Hubbard Radio acquired the station in 2011.

## Format and programming

The format is the classic all-news wheel: news headlines, traffic and weather "on the 8s", business updates and quick-turn interviews, repeated and refreshed continuously. WTOP maintains one of the largest radio newsrooms in the country, with its Glass-Enclosed Nerve Center a minor Washington institution. Federal government coverage is a specialty, and election nights and major political events draw enormous regional audiences.

## Reach

WTOP broadcasts on 103.5 FM across the Washington metropolitan area, with repeaters extending coverage into Maryland and Virginia, and streams via its website and apps. Its revenue leadership — routinely more than $60 million a year — reflects both its audience and the value of the Washington advertising market.

## Significance

WTOP demonstrates that straightforward, well-resourced news radio remains commercially unbeatable when done well. It is the benchmark American all-news station and an essential utility for the capital region's commuters and policymakers alike.
