Radio România Actualități is the main channel of Romania's public broadcaster — the country's radio of record, mixing news, current affairs, service programming and music with nationwide reach that no commercial network matches.

## History

Romanian public radio made its first broadcast on 1 November 1928, opening with the words "Alo, alo, aici Radio București!" ("Hello, hello, this is Radio Bucharest!"). The service grew into the central information channel of Romanian life through monarchy, war, communism — when it was the regime's carefully controlled voice, while Romanians famously tuned foreign broadcasts in secret — and the live-broadcast drama of the 1989 revolution. Reconstituted as a public service broadcaster in the 1990s, Radio România made Actualități ("News/Current Affairs") its flagship generalist channel, flanked by cultural, musical and regional sister networks.

## Format and programming

The schedule runs hourly news bulletins, substantial morning and evening news magazines, political interviews, rural and social affairs programming that serves audiences commercial radio overlooks, and a broad music mix from Romanian pop to folklore. National sports coverage — football above all — remains a core strength.

## Reach

Actualități broadcasts on FM and AM transmitters covering effectively all of Romania, including areas no private network bothers to serve, and streams online for the large Romanian diaspora across Europe.

## Significance

For nearly a century the station has been the baseline of Romanian broadcasting — the channel of solemn announcements, revolutions and football finals alike. It remains among the country's most-listened stations and its most complete public information service.
