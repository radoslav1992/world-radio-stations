Europa FM is one of Romania's major national commercial radio stations, combining an adult contemporary music format with regular news and current affairs programming. Since its launch it has been among the most-listened private stations in the country.

## History

Europa FM went on air in 2000, holding one of the first licences that allowed a private broadcaster to cover Romania with a genuinely national FM network. The station was developed by Lagardère Active Radio International, the international radio arm of France's Lagardère group, which built a portfolio of stations across Central and Eastern Europe. In the late 2010s Lagardère sold its Central European radio businesses, and Europa FM passed to new ownership as part of that regional divestment, continuing to operate as one of Romania's top commercial networks.

## Format and programming

The station targets adult listeners with a playlist blending international and Romanian pop, rock and classic hits. Music programming is complemented by hourly news bulletins from its Bucharest newsroom, an information-rich morning show, and interview and debate strands covering politics and society. This combination of mainstream music and credible journalism has been central to Europa FM's identity since its launch, distinguishing it from purely music-driven rivals.

## Reach

Europa FM broadcasts across Romania through a nationwide network of FM transmitters covering the large cities and much of the country, and is available worldwide through online streaming on its website and mobile apps, along with podcasts of its speech programming.

## Significance

As one of the first private stations with true national coverage in Romania, Europa FM played an important role in the development of the country's post-1989 commercial radio market. It remains a leading audience force in Romanian radio and a significant source of news for listeners who prefer a music-led station.
