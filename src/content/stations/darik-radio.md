Darik Radio is Bulgaria's leading private news and talk radio network. Based in Sofia and broadcasting to cities across the country, it has been a major independent voice in Bulgarian journalism since the early years of the country's democratic transition.

## History

Darik Radio was founded in Sofia in 1993, making it one of the first private radio stations to emerge in Bulgaria after the end of communist rule. Established under businessman Radosvet Radev, it distinguished itself early on with an emphasis on news and political talk at a time when most new private stations chose music formats. Over the following decades Darik built a network of FM frequencies covering Bulgaria's major cities and developed an accompanying online news operation, Dariknews, extending the brand into digital media.

## Format and programming

The station's schedule is dominated by speech: hourly news bulletins, extended morning and drive-time news blocks, political interviews, debates and phone-in discussion. Its programmes regularly feature Bulgarian politicians, economists and analysts, and its interviews are frequently cited by other national media. Sports coverage and selected music and lifestyle strands complement the core news and talk output.

## Reach

Darik Radio broadcasts on FM in Sofia and in cities and regions throughout Bulgaria via its network of local frequencies, giving it near-national coverage among private broadcasters. Its live stream and on-demand audio are available through its website and apps, alongside the Dariknews online news portal.

## Significance

As the country's foremost private news radio network, Darik Radio has played a significant role in Bulgarian public life since the 1990s, providing an independent counterpart to the public broadcaster's Horizont channel. Its longevity and consistent news focus have made it one of the most recognisable media brands in Bulgaria.
