Virgin Radio UK is a national digital music station owned by News UK through its Wireless division, broadcasting the Virgin Radio brand under licence from Virgin Group. Its output mixes contemporary hits with classic pop and rock for an adult audience.

## History

The original Virgin Radio launched nationally in the UK on 30 April 1993 but was rebranded as Absolute Radio after its sale in 2008, leaving the name dormant in British radio. The current station launched on 30 March 2016 as part of a trio of new national digital stations from the Wireless Group, which News UK acquired later that year. The station gained major prominence in January 2019 when Chris Evans, one of Britain's best-known broadcasters, moved from BBC Radio 2 to present its breakfast show. Spin-off stations, including decades- and genre-themed variants such as Virgin Radio Anthems and Virgin Radio Chilled, have extended the brand on digital platforms.

## Format and programming

Virgin Radio UK plays an adult-leaning blend of current pop and pop-rock alongside familiar hits from recent decades. The Chris Evans Breakfast Show is its flagship programme, supported by daytime and weekend shows from established radio and television personalities. Live sessions, artist interviews and video content produced from its London studios form a significant part of its output.

## Reach

The station broadcasts nationally on DAB digital radio and streams online and through its app; it has no analogue FM or AM frequency. The arrival of its high-profile breakfast show substantially raised the station's audience and visibility within UK commercial radio.

## Significance

Virgin Radio UK's relaunch demonstrated the viability of building a national music brand on digital-only distribution, and its recruitment of Chris Evans was one of the most notable presenter moves in recent British radio history. Within News UK it complements talkSPORT and Times Radio in a growing national audio portfolio.
