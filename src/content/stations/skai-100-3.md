Skai 100.3 is one of Greece's foremost private news and talk radio stations. Broadcasting on 100.3 FM in the Athens area, it forms the radio arm of the Skai media group, which also operates the national Skai TV television channel and associated news outlets.

## History

Skai Radio launched in 1989, in the first wave of private broadcasting that followed the liberalisation of the Greek airwaves in the late 1980s. It was founded within the media interests of the Alafouzos family, and quickly established itself as a serious journalistic voice in a crowded new market. The Skai brand later expanded into television and digital news, making the radio station the founding pillar of one of Greece's most prominent private media groups.

## Format and programming

Skai 100.3 is a speech-driven station centred on news bulletins, morning and drive-time news magazines, political interviews and debate. Its schedule features prominent Greek journalists and commentators covering domestic politics, the economy and international affairs, alongside sports coverage and discussion. The station shares newsroom resources with Skai TV and the group's online news operations, allowing extensive coverage of breaking stories.

## Reach

The station's primary signal serves Athens and the wider Attica region on 100.3 FM, with additional frequencies and affiliated relays extending coverage to other parts of Greece. Its programming is also streamed online and distributed as on-demand audio through the Skai group's digital platforms.

## Significance

Skai 100.3 is a reference point for news radio in Greece and a significant force in shaping public debate, particularly in the capital. As the original outlet of the Skai group, it also holds a notable place in the history of private broadcasting in the country following deregulation.
