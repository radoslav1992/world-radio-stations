NRK P1 is the principal radio channel of Norsk rikskringkasting (NRK), Norway's public broadcaster. A broad, full-service channel aimed at a general adult audience, it combines national news, regional programming from NRK's district offices and adult-oriented music, and has long been the most listened-to radio channel in Norway.

## History

P1 traces its lineage directly to the original NRK radio service, which began broadcasting when the corporation was founded in 1933 and for decades was Norway's only national radio channel. The channel took on the name P1 as NRK expanded its radio portfolio, particularly with the launch of the youth channel P3 in the 1990s, which prompted a clearer division of roles: P1 became the mainstream channel for adult listeners, P2 the culture and current affairs channel. NRK P1's editorial base is in Trondheim, making it unusual among European flagship channels in being headquartered outside the capital.

## Format and programming

P1 operates as a full-service network. National news bulletins from the NRK newsroom run throughout the day, complemented by weather and traffic information, magazine shows, sport and light entertainment. A defining feature is its regional programming: NRK's district offices around the country provide local news and shows in daytime windows, giving the channel a strong local presence from Finnmark to Agder. The music profile is broad and adult-oriented, mixing Norwegian and international pop, classics and easy-listening material. The channel also plays a central role in Norway's emergency broadcasting arrangements.

## Reach

Following Norway's pioneering switch-off of national FM broadcasting in 2017, P1 is distributed nationwide on DAB+ digital radio, and it is also available online and via the NRK Radio app. It consistently records the largest audience share of any Norwegian radio channel.

## Significance

As the direct descendant of Norway's first national radio service and the country's dominant channel, NRK P1 is a cornerstone of Norwegian public broadcasting, valued for its news coverage, regional roots and role in public emergency communication.
