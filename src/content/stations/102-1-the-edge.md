102.1 the Edge is Toronto's alternative rock station and one of the most storied frequencies in Canadian music. Under its original identity CFNY, it was the free-spirited broadcaster whose slogan — "The Spirit of Radio" — Rush borrowed for their 1980 classic of the same name.

## History

CFNY grew out of a small Brampton FM licence in the 1970s and became famous for playing what no one else in Canada would: punk, new wave, post-punk and electronic imports, programmed with almost total DJ freedom. Its "Spirit of Radio" era made it a legend — literally so when Rush, admirers of the station, titled a song after its slogan. CFNY founded the CASBY Awards (originally the U-Knows) to honour the independent music it championed, and through the 1990s, rebranded as 102.1 the Edge, it was the national tastemaker for Canadian alternative, giving early support to bands from Barenaked Ladies to Billy Talent, whom it helped sign to prominence.

## Format and programming

The Edge plays modern and classic alternative rock with a strong Canadian tilt, supported by the country's content rules but driven by genuine scene involvement — new-music shows, live sessions and its long association with Toronto's club and festival circuit.

## Reach

Broadcasting at 102.1 FM across the Greater Toronto Area and streaming nationally, the Edge remains the reference alternative station in English Canada.

## Significance

Few stations anywhere have a resume like CFNY's: it imported new wave to Canada, inspired one of rock's great songs about radio itself, created a national music award, and launched a generation of Canadian alternative acts. The Edge carries that lineage into the streaming era.
