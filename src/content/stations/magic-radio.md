Magic Radio is a mellow adult contemporary station from London, playing "more of the songs you love" — familiar pop classics, soul and singalong favourites for listeners who want warmth rather than edge. Owned by Bauer, it pairs a heritage London FM signal with national digital reach.

## History

The 105.4 FM frequency launched as Melody FM in 1990, an unabashedly easy-listening service for London. Rebranded Magic 105.4 in 1998 under EMAP, it sharpened into a modern melodic AC station and became a persistent challenger at the top of the London ratings — at various points the capital's most-listened commercial station. Bauer, which inherited the brand from EMAP, took Magic national on digital radio in 2015 and built out spin-offs including Mellow Magic and Magic Soul.

## Format and programming

Magic's playlist runs from the Bee Gees and Diana Ross to Adele and George Ezra, presented with a light, friendly touch. The station has carved out a distinctive niche in show tunes and musical theatre — its "Magic at the Musicals" concerts with West End casts are an annual fixture at the Royal Albert Hall — and its Christmas programming is famously full-hearted.

## Reach

Magic broadcasts on 105.4 FM across Greater London and nationally on DAB, with its digital sister stations extending the brand. Streaming and the Magic app carry it well beyond the capital.

## Significance

Magic proved that unashamedly gentle radio could win the biggest city in Britain. Its balance of nostalgia and polish — plus its singular connection to London's theatreland — gives it a personality quite unlike any other UK music network.
