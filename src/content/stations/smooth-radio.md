Smooth Radio is the United Kingdom's leading relaxing-music station, playing familiar soul, Motown, soft pop and timeless ballads for an adult audience. Under Global's ownership it has grown into one of the largest commercial radio brands in the country.

## History

The Smooth brand passed through several hands — including Guardian Media Group's regional Smooth stations of the 2000s — before Global acquired it and relaunched Smooth nationally in 2014 around a simple promise: music to relax to. The repositioning proved enormously effective, and Smooth climbed steadily up the UK ratings to sit alongside Global's other giants, Heart and Capital, with decade- and genre-based spin-offs following on digital platforms.

## Format and programming

The playlist leans on the most-loved records of the past five decades — Motown and soul staples, soft rock, and ballads from artists like George Michael, Whitney Houston and Elton John — presented at an unhurried pace. Well-known presenters front the key shows, with Jenni Falconer waking the network up at breakfast, and features like the "Smooth Sanctuary at Seven" lean into the brand's promise of calm.

## Reach

Smooth broadcasts on FM across many UK regions — including 102.2 in London — and nationally on DAB, with streaming through Global Player. Its weekly audience places it consistently among Britain's top commercial networks.

## Significance

Smooth demonstrated the scale of demand for genuinely relaxed music radio in a market crowded with hit formats. Its success pushed "relaxing" from a niche daypart concept to a national brand position, and it now anchors the calmer end of Global's portfolio the way Capital anchors the energetic end.
