98.1 CHFI is Toronto's heritage adult contemporary station and one of the most consistently successful radio stations in Canada. Warm, melodic and deliberately easy company, it has spent much of the past three decades at or near number one in the country's biggest market.

## History

CHFI-FM signed on in 1957, a genuine FM pioneer in Canada at a time when the band was an afterthought — its call letters even nod to "high fidelity". Under Rogers, whose broadcasting empire grew around it, CHFI evolved from beautiful music into the polished adult contemporary format it has carried since the 1980s. Erin Davis, its beloved morning co-host across three decades, became one of Canadian radio's defining voices before retiring in 2016; the morning show has remained Toronto's ratings benchmark since.

## Format and programming

CHFI plays soft pop and AC favourites from the 1980s to today, built around a big-hearted morning show, workplace-friendly daytimes and light entertainment features. Its annual switch to all-Christmas music each holiday season is a Toronto tradition that reliably rockets the station to enormous ratings.

## Reach

Broadcasting at 98.1 FM from atop the CN Tower area transmitters, CHFI covers the Greater Toronto Area — a market of some six million people — and streams nationally through Rogers' platforms.

## Significance

CHFI is the model Canadian AC station: technically superb, relentlessly consistent and woven into daily life in Canada's largest city. Its longevity at the top of Toronto's ratings, against every format fashion of the past forty years, is one of North American radio's quiet dynasties.
