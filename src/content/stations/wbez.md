WBEZ is the leading public radio station in Chicago, broadcasting on 91.5 FM as a member of NPR. Operated by Chicago Public Media, it pairs national public radio programming with a substantial local newsroom covering the city and the wider region.

## History

WBEZ signed on in 1943 as a station of the Chicago Board of Education, initially delivering instructional programming to schools. It later transitioned into a community-licensed public station and became an NPR member, shifting decisively toward news and talk. The station earned a national reputation as an incubator of new radio storytelling: This American Life originated at WBEZ in the 1990s, and the station's production culture later contributed to the podcast era, including the widely known series Serial, which grew out of This American Life. In recent years Chicago Public Media has expanded beyond radio, notably by acquiring the Chicago Sun-Times newspaper in 2022.

## Format and programming

WBEZ follows a news and talk format built around NPR programs such as Morning Edition and All Things Considered, alongside locally produced news, interview and current-affairs shows. Its journalists cover Chicago politics, education, criminal justice and culture, and the station produces podcasts and digital reporting in addition to its broadcast schedule.

## Reach

The station serves the Chicago metropolitan area on 91.5 FM and reaches listeners nationwide and internationally through online streaming, its website and podcast distribution.

## Significance

WBEZ is one of the most influential public radio stations in the United States. Its role in launching nationally distributed programs helped redefine narrative audio journalism, and its combination with the Sun-Times has made Chicago Public Media a prominent experiment in sustaining local news through nonprofit ownership.
