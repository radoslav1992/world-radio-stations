BBC Radio 5 Live is the BBC's national radio network for live news and sport. Its schedule mixes breaking news, interviews and listener phone-ins with commentary on football, cricket, rugby, tennis and other major sporting events.

## History

5 Live launched on 28 March 1994, replacing the earlier BBC Radio 5, which had run since 1990 as a mixed sport and education network. The relaunch created a dedicated rolling news and sport service, partly in response to the BBC's experience of covering the 1991 Gulf War. In 2011 the station moved from London to MediaCityUK in Salford as part of the BBC's relocation of departments to the north of England. A sister station, 5 Sports Extra, launched in 2002 to carry additional live sports coverage on digital platforms.

## Format and programming

Weekday output is built around live news sequences, with breakfast and drivetime programmes, extended interviews and audience phone-ins that make the station a prominent forum for national debate. Sport dominates evenings and weekends, with Premier League and other football commentaries, coverage of major tournaments, and long-running discussion programmes. The station is also a significant producer of sport and news podcasts distributed through BBC Sounds.

## Reach

5 Live broadcasts across the United Kingdom on medium wave frequencies of 693 and 909 kHz and on DAB digital radio, and is available online through BBC Sounds. Rights restrictions mean some live sports commentaries are limited to UK listeners.

## Significance

As the UK's principal live news and sport radio network, 5 Live plays a major role in national coverage of breaking stories and sporting occasions. Its phone-in culture helped establish interactive talk as a mainstay of British radio, and its Salford base made it a cornerstone of the BBC's presence outside London.
