Cadena COPE is one of Spain's largest national radio networks, offering a generalist schedule of news, opinion, entertainment and sport. Its name derives from "Cadena de Ondas Populares Españolas", and it is distinguished among the big Spanish networks by its ownership links to the Catholic Church.

## History

COPE grew out of the network of "Radio Popular" stations established by the Catholic Church in Spain from the mid-twentieth century, when dioceses and religious institutions operated local broadcasters across the country. These stations were progressively unified under the COPE brand, and the network developed into a full national competitor to Cadena SER during the 1980s and 1990s. Its principal shareholders remain institutions of the Spanish Catholic Church, including the Spanish Episcopal Conference, and the network sits at the centre of a media group that also includes music and sports radio brands.

## Format and programming

COPE follows the Spanish generalist model of long magazine programmes anchored by star presenters. Weekday mornings are led by its flagship news and talk show, while afternoons and evenings mix current affairs, interviews and entertainment. Sport is a defining strength: the weekend football marathon "Tiempo de Juego" and the nightly sports programme "El Partidazo de COPE" are among the most popular sports shows on Spanish radio. Religious programming, including Mass and Church-related content, reflects the network's ownership.

## Reach

The network broadcasts throughout Spain via an extensive chain of FM and AM frequencies, combining owned stations with local affiliates that insert regional programming. Live streams, on-demand audio and podcasts are available through its website and apps.

## Significance

COPE is consistently one of the two most-listened generalist networks in Spain, and its presenters and sports broadcasts hold a prominent place in Spanish popular culture. It represents a rare example in Europe of a Church-linked broadcaster competing at the top of the national commercial radio market.
