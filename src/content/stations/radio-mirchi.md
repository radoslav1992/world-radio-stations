Mirchi — long known as Radio Mirchi — is India's largest private radio brand, the sound of Bollywood on FM across the subcontinent's great cities. Its name means "chilli", its longtime tagline promised "It's hot!", and for a hundred million-plus urban Indians its RJs are daily companions.

## History

Radio Mirchi launched in 2001, when India first allowed private FM broadcasting at scale, with Entertainment Network India — part of the Times of India group — winning licences across the country. Standardizing on 98.3 FM wherever possible, Mirchi built the first truly national private radio brand in India, riding the growth of urban FM through the 2000s licence expansions into more than sixty stations. In 2020 the company rebranded simply as "Mirchi", reflecting a business now spanning digital audio, video and live events as much as broadcast.

## Format and programming

Programming centres on Bollywood film music — new soundtracks, retro gold and everything the charts love — delivered in Hindi and regional languages by celebrity RJs whose banter, pranks and interviews are as much the product as the songs. Mirchi's Mumbai studios sit at the heart of the film industry, giving it unrivalled star access, and its Mirchi Music Awards are a fixture of the Bollywood calendar. Sister stations serve retro, romantic and regional formats.

## Reach

Mirchi broadcasts in cities across India and streams globally to the diaspora, complementing FM with original podcasts and YouTube content at enormous scale.

## Significance

Mirchi built the template for Indian private radio: film music plus personality, localized across a dozen languages. As FM remains a mass medium in India's cities, Mirchi is its defining brand.
