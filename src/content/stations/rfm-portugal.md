RFM is Portugal's ratings champion — a national hit music station that has spent most of the past three decades as the most-listened radio brand in the country. Bright, melodic and relentlessly professional, it is the commercial jewel of the Renascença media group.

## History

RFM launched in 1987 as the music-driven sister of Rádio Renascença, the historic Catholic broadcaster whose place in Portuguese life was already legendary (its airing of "Grândola, Vila Morena" famously signalled the start of the 1974 Carnation Revolution). Freed from the parent station's broader obligations, RFM pursued a pure music format and steadily climbed to national leadership, overtaking its elder sibling and holding off all commercial challengers since.

## Format and programming

The station plays contemporary hits and recent favourites, Portuguese and international, wrapped in slick production and a famously strong morning show, "Café da Manhã". Its playlist discipline is matched by big promotional swings — flash-mob stunts, listener road trips and RFM SOMNII, the massive summer electronic beach festival in Figueira da Foz that the station built into one of Portugal's largest music events.

## Reach

RFM broadcasts on FM across mainland Portugal and the islands and streams worldwide, where it serves the large Portuguese diaspora; weekly audiences regularly top two million in a country of ten million.

## Significance

RFM shows how a legacy religious broadcaster grew a thoroughly modern market leader without diluting either brand. For advertisers and artists alike, it is simply the biggest stage in Portuguese radio.
