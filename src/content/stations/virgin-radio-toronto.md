Virgin Radio 99.9 is Toronto's flagship hit music station, playing current pop and recent favourites for a young-skewing mass audience. Operating on the historic CKFM frequency, it was the first station in North America to carry the Virgin Radio brand.

## History

The 99.9 frequency has been a Toronto fixture since the middle of the twentieth century under the CKFM call letters, spending years as the adult contemporary "Mix 99.9". In 2006 owner Standard Broadcasting licensed the Virgin Radio name from Richard Branson's Virgin Group and relaunched the station as Virgin Radio 999 — bringing the famous British brand across the Atlantic for the first time and pointing the format squarely at pop hits. Astral Media and then Bell Media inherited the station, and Bell has since rolled the Virgin Radio brand out to cities across Canada, with Toronto as the mothership.

## Format and programming

Virgin plays contemporary hits and rhythmic pop with high-energy personality shows around breakfast and the drive home, celebrity and entertainment content, and heavy involvement in Toronto's pop culture calendar — from Jingle Ball-style concerts to artist meet-and-greets.

## Reach

The station's 99.9 FM signal covers the Greater Toronto Area, with streaming through iHeartRadio Canada, and it consistently ranks among the leaders for young listeners in Canada's largest market.

## Significance

Virgin Radio 99.9 turned a heritage Toronto frequency into the beachhead for one of radio's most successful international brand franchises. As the template for Bell's national Virgin network, it shaped how pop radio sounds in practically every major Canadian city.
